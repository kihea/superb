About

Most people stopped reading. Superb helps you start again.

Superb is a reading app that builds your vocabulary. You read real books, tap the words you do not know, and play short games with them. Your vocabulary grows while you read.

Reading comes first

You can open the app at night and read without any pressure. Superb saves your place in every book while you scroll. Ordinary reading is private: Superb records your place and the words you keep, and nothing else.

Tap a word for its meaning

Tap a word while you read. A small card opens on the page with its meaning. Keep the word if you like it, and Superb saves it to your words page with the sentence you found it in.

Three games

Rhyme shows you a word. Type or say words that rhyme with it. You get a bonus for putting them in one sentence. Rhyme builds short-term recall.

Association shows you a word. Give the words that relate to it. At the end you see your answers next to the ones you missed.

Prose writes passages for you. The sentences are long, the meanings are not obvious, and some words are new. This is the only place where the learning engine chooses what you see.

What it costs

Nothing. Every book is public domain, the code is source-available, and you make no account. If it is free to supply, it is free to you.

What it takes from you

Nothing leaves your device. Your shelf, your places, your kept words and the engine's state all live in your own browser's storage. Superb does not track you and runs no analytics. The app asks the network for one thing only: the book you opened.

Superb is an open project. If something reads wrong or breaks, tell us on GitHub. That is how Superb improves.
